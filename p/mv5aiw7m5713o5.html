Retin-A is a prescription topical retinoid, a vitamin A derivative. It is used to treat acne: it helps clear and prevent pimples, blackheads and clogged pores by speeding up skin cell turnover. Doctors also prescribe it for fine wrinkles, uneven texture and some signs of sun damage.

How to use: cream, apply a thin layer to clean, dry skin in the evening. The schedule is set by a doctor, and the skin usually needs a few weeks to adjust.
