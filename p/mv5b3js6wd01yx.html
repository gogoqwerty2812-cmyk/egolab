Aklief is a prescription topical retinoid with trifarotene 0.005% (50 micrograms per gram). It is used to treat acne on the face and on the trunk (chest, shoulders and back). It works on the retinoid receptor that is most common in the skin, which helps clear and prevent pimples and blackheads, reduces inflammation and helps with marks left after acne.

How to use: cream, apply once a day in the evening to clean, dry skin. Prime the pump with up to 10 presses before the first use. Use 1 pump for the face, 2 for the upper trunk and 1 extra for the middle or lower back. Do not use more than 4 pumps a day. Use a moisturiser from the start of treatment.
