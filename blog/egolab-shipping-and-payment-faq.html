EgoLab FAQ: shipping, payment by card or crypto, and how ordering works

12 Nov 2026 · 2 min read · EgoLab

EgoLab is an online sports nutrition store: protein, creatine, pre-workout, vitamins and gym accessories, with lab reports for our products. This page answers the questions we get most about delivery and payment.

Where we ship

We deliver to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Poland, Serbia, Bulgaria and Czechia. In the US you choose your state at checkout.

Shipping cost

  • Flat $17 per order.
  • Free shipping from $99 — calculated on the order total after any promo discount.

Prices on the site are in US dollars. For delivery times to your country, message us before ordering and we will give you an estimate.

Paying by card

Choose Bank card at checkout. You are taken to our secure payment partner to pay with Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay or Google Pay; PayPal is offered in some regions. We never see your card details. The partner processes the payment as a crypto purchase, so the page may say "Buy crypto" — you are only charged the order total. As soon as the payment goes through, your order is confirmed automatically.

Paying with crypto

Choose Cryptocurrency, pick a coin and network, and you will see our wallet address with a QR code. Send the exact total, then paste the transaction hash at checkout so we can match your payment.

Always send on the network you selected. Sending USDT on the wrong network can lose the funds. With Monero, no transaction hash is needed — because Monero transfers are private, we confirm the payment directly in our wallet.

How to place an order

  1. Add products to your cart and apply a promo code if you have one.
  2. Enter your delivery address and at least one contact: phone, email, WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Facebook or Signal.
  3. Choose card or crypto and complete the payment.
  4. Agree to the processing of your data for the order and place it. You get an order number right away.

No account is needed. We use your details only to process, ship and support the order.

Quality

We publish third-party lab reports on our Lab reports page. Not sure how to read one? See how to read a supplement lab report.

Contact

Email egolab@mailfence.com or Telegram @egolab_shop, or use the support page.

Frequently asked questions

Which countries does EgoLab ship to?

The United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France, Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain, Portugal, Italy, Poland, Serbia, Bulgaria and Czechia.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is a flat $17. Orders of $99 or more (after any promo discount) ship free.

Can I pay by card?

Yes. Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted through our secure payment partner; PayPal is available in some regions. The order is confirmed automatically once the payment goes through.

Which cryptocurrencies can I pay with?

Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Tether (USDT on TRON, BNB Smart Chain or Solana), BNB, Solana (SOL), TRON (TRX), Toncoin (TON), Monero (XMR), Litecoin (LTC) and Dogecoin (DOGE).

Which crypto network is cheapest to pay with?

USDT on BNB Smart Chain or Solana, and coins such as SOL, TRX, TON, LTC, DOGE and XMR usually cost only cents to send. Bitcoin and Ethereum network fees are higher.

Do I need an account to order?

No. Checkout works without an account. You only need a delivery address and at least one way for us to contact you — phone, email or a messenger.
